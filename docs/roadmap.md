Core M1–M3 capabilities and the M4 evaluation tool have landed; acceptance still depends on the seven-day, recovery-time, and labeled-cat checks above. Device deployment is coordinated sequentially. Every PR needs meaningful tests, static checks, CI validation, and at least two read-only reviews; unresolved BLOCKING/IMPORTANT findings prevent merge.

For M1, reloading the Pi UI in Safari showed 989 saved snapshots, latest saved at 16:48:45 JST with one detection, the no-target retention explanation, a separate live image updating about every five seconds, and healthy/ready status. The saved count and latest timestamp matched the API. This final UI check was completed on 2026-10-11 at 18:14 JST.

Seven-day observation started on 2026-10-11 12:02:49 JST. The result timer is scheduled for 2026-10-18 12:03 JST; tool completion and sample ratios do not establish time uptime. Cat accuracy remains unevaluated until real labeled images are available; synthetic fixtures verify the evaluator only. Record acceptance thresholds before measurement and preserve baselines.

Webhook, video upload, camera-aware management, and tracking have server implementations. Notification delivery, ESP-side short clips, multiple physical cameras, and tracking still need real-device validation. After the foundations pass, plan these as separate Issues. NAS/Drive/S3, Tapo integration, and moving hardware remain future work requiring explicit interface and operational decisions.
